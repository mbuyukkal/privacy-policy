Dilki Vocabulary Crosswords

Last updated: July 25, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Dilki Vocabulary Crosswords (“the App”, “we”, “us”) handles information when you use our mobile application.

Your Progress Stays On Your Device

All profiles you create, your selected level and grid size preferences, your in-progress puzzles, and your hint balance are stored locally on your device only. We do not operate any server, do not require an account, and never see or receive this data ourselves. If you delete the App, this data is deleted with it. You can also remove a single profile at any time from the Profiles screen by long-pressing it and confirming deletion.

Hints & In-App Purchases

The App includes an optional hint system. Hint packs are one-time, non-renewing in-app purchases made through the Apple App Store or Google Play billing system; we do not receive or store your payment details. Because your hint balance is tied to your device rather than an individual profile, it is shared across every profile on that device.

Advertising

The App shows interstitial and rewarded video ads through Google's AdMob service to support free access to the App. To provide and measure these ads, Google's Mobile Ads SDK and User Messaging Platform SDK may collect information such as your device's advertising identifier, IP-based approximate location, and ad interaction data. This data is collected and processed by Google, not by us — we never receive or see it directly. You can review how Google handles this information in Google's Privacy Policy and the AdMob-specific disclosures.

Subscriptions

The App offers an optional monthly or annual subscription to remove ads. Subscriptions are purchased and managed entirely through the Apple App Store or Google Play Store billing system; we do not receive or store your payment details.

App Tracking Transparency

On first launch, iOS may ask for your permission to allow tracking for personalized ads. If you decline, the App continues to work exactly the same — you'll simply see non-personalized ads instead.

Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children of that age.

Changes to This Policy

If our data practices ever change, this policy will be updated and the “Last updated” date above will reflect the change.

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at: mbuyukkal@gmail.com
