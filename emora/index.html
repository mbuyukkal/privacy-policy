This Privacy Policy describes how Emora ("the App", "we", "us") handles information when you use our mobile application.

No Data Collection

We do not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data or usage data from users of this App. The App does not require an account, does not connect to any server operated by us, and does not use analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

Permissions

If the App requests any device permissions, they are used solely to provide the App's core functionality on your device and are never transmitted anywhere.

Third-Party Services

The App does not integrate any third-party SDKs that collect data (such as analytics, advertising, or crash-reporting tools that transmit information off your device).

Children's Privacy

Since we do not collect any data, the App does not knowingly collect personal information from children of any age.

Changes to This Policy

If our data practices ever change, this policy will be updated and the "Last updated" date above will reflect the change.
